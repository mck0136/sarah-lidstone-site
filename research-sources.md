## Verified But Used Conservatively

- University Health Network, University of Toronto, Toronto Western Hospital, Toronto Rehabilitation Institute, and the Integrated Movement Disorders Program appear in PubMed author affiliations. The site shows these as publication-supported affiliations, but current status should be confirmed.
- TEDxUofT is verified by the official TEDx Talks YouTube title and metadata.
- "Integrated Rehabilitation" appears in the official TEDx description. The site uses this cautiously and flags it for approval.
