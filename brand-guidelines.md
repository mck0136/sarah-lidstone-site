# Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Primary line: `Neurology that sees the whole person.`

The brand should feel editorial, medically credible, calm, feminine, firm, and human. It should avoid clinic marketing tropes and instead read like a thoughtful physician-scientist and speaker profile.

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm ivory | `#FCFAF8` | Primary page background |
| Dusty blush | `#DFAFB2` | Structural accents, side panels, quiet callouts |
| Oxblood | `#6E2427` | Buttons, rules, key labels, authority accents |
| Deep slate navy | `#263957` | Navigation, metadata, major text |
| Soft charcoal | `#222222` | Body copy |
| Pale warm grey | `#E9E4E0` | Dividers and quiet backgrounds |

Avoid bright pink, purple, electric blue, teal gradients, wellness greens, generic hospital blue, and one-note luxury palettes.

## Typography

- Headlines: `Cormorant Garamond`, with Georgia fallback.
- Body, navigation, forms, and publication metadata: `Inter`, with Arial fallback.
- Script styling is reserved only for the `SL` monogram treatment. Do not use script fonts for headings, navigation, buttons, or body copy.

## Spacing System

- Mobile page padding: 20px.
- Desktop page padding: 32px.
- Section spacing: 64px baseline.
- Cards: 24px padding, 4px border radius.
- Use generous whitespace, but keep operational sections dense enough to scan.

## Logo And Monogram

Temporary system:

- Thin circular `SL` monogram.
- Formal wordmark: `DR. SARAH LIDSTONE`.
- Descriptor: `Neurologist - Researcher - Speaker`.

Use the monogram in the header, footer, favicon, and occasional hero or speaker assets. Do not overuse it as a pattern or decorative flourish.

## Buttons

Primary:

- Oxblood background.
- White text.
- Border radius 4px.
- Strong focus state.

Secondary:

- Ivory or transparent background.
- Oxblood border and text.
- Subtle blush hover.

Quiet:

- Text link style with oxblood and underline on hover.

## Photography Direction

Use a professional portrait that feels warm, direct, and intelligent. Ideal image qualities:

- Natural expression.
- Clean academic/editorial lighting.
- Neutral or softly warm background.
- No stethoscope-centered stock styling.
- No generic hospital corridor imagery.
- No glowing brain graphics.
- No flowers, watercolors, spa visuals, or wellness cliches.

Until approved photography is available, use the restrained abstract movement/connection visual provided in the site.

## What Not To Do

- Do not imply private patient availability.
- Do not use patient testimonials or outcomes marketing.
- Do not call any treatment `proprietary`, `breakthrough`, or `novel` unless explicitly approved and sourced.
- Do not suggest symptoms are imagined or "all in someone's head."
- Do not use generic hospital imagery, glowing neurological stock art, or decorative feminine motifs.
- Do not add busy animation, carousels, or visual noise.
